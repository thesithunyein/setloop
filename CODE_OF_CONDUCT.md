# Code of conduct

The Setloop project welcomes contributors and players of every background and experience level. Treat people with respect in issues, pull requests, discussions, and project spaces.

- Give specific feedback about the work and be open to corrections.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and experience levels.
- Get permission before sharing someone's private information, messages, or player link.
- Keep the community free of harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and personal attacks.

## Reporting and moderation

Contact the [maintainer](https://github.com/thesithunyein) privately to report a concern. If you need a private contact channel, ask for one through the repository without naming anyone or posting evidence. Share the relevant context and any immediate concerns once you have a private channel.

The maintainer will review reports, limit sharing of identifying details, and take action appropriate to the circumstances. Actions can include a warning, removal of content, or limits on participation.
